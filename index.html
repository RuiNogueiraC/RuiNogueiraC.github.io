01 About Me

I work where IT meets cyber security — equally comfortable keeping systems running smoothly and digging into how they can be attacked, monitored, or hardened.

My focus is on the practical side of security: understanding how incidents happen, how to respond to them, and how good IT processes prevent most of them from happening in the first place.

Outside of work, I build things with my hands — DIY electronics projects, small hardware hacks.
